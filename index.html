The name

A bee that finds something good doesn’t keep it to itself.

She flies home and dances. The angle of the dance tells the hive which way to go. How long it lasts tells them how far. Biologists call it the waggle dance.

That is the whole idea of Waggle. Go out, find what matters, come back and tell me.

angle of the run = which waystraight up = toward the sun

Why we’re building it

Every AI I’ve used has the same flaw. It waits.

It only knows what I type into it. It forgets me when the window closes. It never notices the deadline, the broken build or the one email that mattered.

So we’re building the one I actually wanted: an assistant that behaves like someone who has worked with me for years.

Different, part one

It speaks first, and it knows when not to.

Three times a day Waggle looks at the whole picture: my notes, my projects, my mail, the weather, today’s date, what it already told me and how I reacted. Then it asks itself one question. Is anything here worth interrupting me for?

Usually the answer is no. That restraint is the feature.

How it's built

One voice note, end to end.

  • Watchdog

    A second process checks the core every 10 seconds. Killed on purpose, it was back in 57 seconds.

  • Timers

    54 scheduled timers, each with its own catch-up rule for when the PC was switched off.

  • Jobs

    Background work holds a lease tied to process id plus start time, because Windows reuses process ids.

  • Records

    One trace per turn: who really answered, every tool call and its result, timings, outcome, code version.
